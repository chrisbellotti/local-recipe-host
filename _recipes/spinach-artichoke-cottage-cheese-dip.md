## Ingredients

- 1 tub (16 oz) 2% cottage cheese
- 1/4 cup parmesan cheese, grated
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- 3 cups fresh spinach
- 1 cup canned artichoke hearts (in water), drained and chopped

## Instructions

1. Wilt the spinach in a pan over medium heat or in the microwave for about 1 minute.
2. Let the spinach cool slightly, squeeze out as much water as you can, and chop it.
3. Add the parmesan, lemon juice, olive oil, garlic powder, red pepper flakes, salt and pepper to the cottage cheese tub.
4. Blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
5. Stir in the spinach and artichoke hearts until evenly mixed.

## Pairs well with

- Pita chips, crackers or toasted baguette
- Carrots, celery and bell pepper
- Spread on a chicken or turkey sandwich
- Warmed and spooned over a baked potato

## Notes

- Amounts are estimated; the source only listed the ingredients.
- Squeeze the spinach well, or the dip will turn watery.
- Serve cold, or warm it in a microwave-safe dish in 30-second bursts, stirring between each.
- Most 16 oz tubs are filled close to the top. If there isn't room, blend in the tub and stir in the spinach and artichokes in a bowl.
