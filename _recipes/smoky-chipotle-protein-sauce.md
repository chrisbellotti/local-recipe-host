## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp chipotle powder
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Add the yogurt, chipotle powder, paprika, garlic powder, honey, lemon juice and salt to a small bowl.
2. Whisk until smooth and evenly colored.
3. Whisk in the water to loosen it.
4. Taste and adjust, then chill for 15 minutes so the chipotle blooms.

## Pairs well with

- Burrito bowls and steak or chicken quesadillas
- Sweet potato fries or wedges, as a dip
- Spread on [Sheet Pan Breakfast Sandwiches]({{ '/recipes/sheet-pan-breakfast-sandwiches/' | relative_url }}) in place of the sweet and spicy sauce
- Smash burgers or turkey burgers

## Notes

- The source lists only ingredients; the method is written from them.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
