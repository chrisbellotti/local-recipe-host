## Ingredients

- 1 small ripe banana
- 1/2 cup natural peanut butter
- 1/2 cup vanilla protein powder
- Chocolate chips, to top (optional)
- Sea salt, to top (optional)

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. Mash the banana, then fold in the peanut butter and protein powder.
3. Let the dough sit for a minute or two, then mix again; the protein will thicken it slightly.
4. Scoop 6 cookies onto the baking sheet. The dough will be sticky, so form circles as best you can.
5. Top with chocolate chips, if using.
6. Press each cookie down lightly with a fork to form discs.
7. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are golden and the center is no longer wet.
8. Sprinkle with sea salt and let cool on the sheet for 5 minutes before moving to a cooling rack.

## Notes

- Best eaten right away; they soften after being stored.
