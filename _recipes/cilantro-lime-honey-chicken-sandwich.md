## Ingredients

### Crispy chicken

- Chicken breast
- Flour
- Egg, beaten
- Corn flakes, crushed

### Cilantro lime sauce

- 1 tbsp avocado oil
- 1/2 cup lime juice
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp cumin
- 3 garlic cloves, chopped
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup cilantro, chopped
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Cucumber salad

- Cucumber, sliced
- Cabbage, sliced
- Jalapeño, sliced
- Honey, to taste
- Pickle juice, to taste
- Onion powder, garlic powder, salt and pepper, to taste
- Lime zest

### To serve

- Ciabatta bread

## Instructions

1. Dredge the chicken in flour, then egg, then crushed corn flakes.
2. Air fry at 385F for 18-20 minutes, flipping halfway, until the chicken reaches 165F inside.
3. In a pan, combine the avocado oil, lime juice, honey, cumin, garlic, water, salt and pepper.
4. Reduce a little over medium heat, then stir in the cilantro.
5. Put the cucumber, cabbage and jalapeño in a jar or container with the honey, pickle juice, onion powder, garlic powder, salt, pepper and lime zest. Shake to combine.
6. Split the ciabatta, add the chicken, spoon over the cilantro lime sauce and top with the cucumber salad.
