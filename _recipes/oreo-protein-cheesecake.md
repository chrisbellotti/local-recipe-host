## Ingredients

### Cheesecake

- 480g plain nonfat Greek yogurt
- 240g 2% cottage cheese
- 1 block (226g) fat-free cream cheese
- 2 large eggs
- 100g egg whites
- 5g vanilla extract
- 100g vanilla whey/casein blend protein powder
- 28g sugar-free, fat-free cheesecake pudding mix
- 20g zero-calorie sweetener
- 8 OREO Thins, crushed

### OREO protein icing

- 20g vanilla whey/casein blend protein powder
- 20g powdered sugar
- 10g black cocoa powder
- Cold water

### To serve

- Whipped cream
- Crushed OREOs

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 300F.
2. Blend all the cheesecake ingredients except the OREOs on low until smooth.
3. Wrap the outside of a 9-inch springform pan with aluminum foil to keep the water bath out.
4. Spray the inside of the pan with nonstick spray and pour in the batter.
5. Stir in the crushed OREO Thins.
6. Set the pan in a sheet pan with sides at least 2 inches high, and pour in warm water to about 1-1.5 inches deep.
7. Bake for 55 minutes.
8. Turn the oven off, prop the door open slightly and leave the cheesecake inside for another 35 minutes.
9. Remove the foil and let the cheesecake cool at room temperature for 1 hour.
10. Refrigerate overnight to fully set.
11. For the icing, mix the protein powder, powdered sugar and black cocoa, then add cold water a little at a time until it reaches an icing consistency.
12. Slice the cheesecake and top each slice with icing, whipped cream and crushed OREOs.

## Notes

- The source lists "8g OREO Thins" but the directions say 8 crushed OREO Thins; 8 cookies is used here.
- Add the water to the icing slowly; too much makes it runny.
- The source doesn't give a slice count; 8 slices is an estimate.
