## Ingredients

- 1 lb spaghetti
- 6 tbsp unsalted butter
- 8 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 1 cup grated parmesan, plus more to serve
- 1/4 cup chopped parsley
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Save 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
2. Melt the butter in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and pepper flakes and cook until the garlic is golden, about 3 minutes.
3. Add the pasta, parmesan, and a splash of pasta water. Toss until glossy, adding more water as needed.
4. Stir in the parsley and season with salt and pepper.

## Notes

- Don't let the garlic brown too far or it turns bitter.
