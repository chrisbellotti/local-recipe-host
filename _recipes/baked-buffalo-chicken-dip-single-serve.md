## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup nonfat plain Greek yogurt
- 1/4 cup 2% cottage cheese
- 1 tbsp buffalo sauce
- Ranch seasoning, to taste
- 2 oz cooked chicken, shredded
- 1 serving (about 1 oz) shredded Mexican cheese blend

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350F.
2. In a small oven-safe dish or ramekin, mix the Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, buffalo sauce and ranch seasoning.
3. Stir in the shredded chicken and spread it into an even layer.
4. Top with the Mexican cheese.
5. Bake for 12-15 minutes, until hot all the way through.
6. Switch the oven to broil on high and broil until the cheese is browned and crispy, watching closely.

## Pairs well with

- Celery, carrots and cucumber
- Tortilla chips or pita chips
- Spooned into lettuce cups or a wrap
- Scaled up in a larger dish for game day

## Notes

- Blend the cottage cheese first if you want a smoother dip.
