## Ingredients

- 1 tub (16 oz) 2% cottage cheese
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 jalapeño, seeded and diced
- 2 green onions, diced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Blend the cottage cheese in its tub with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
2. Scoop in the avocado and add the jalapeño, green onions, garlic, olive oil, salt and pepper.
3. Blend again until combined.
4. Taste, adjust the salt, and chill until ready to serve.

## Pairs well with

- Tortilla chips, pita chips or crackers
- Cucumber, bell pepper and snap peas
- A spread for turkey or chicken wraps
- A topping for tacos or burrito bowls

## Notes

- Amounts are estimated; the source only listed the ingredients.
- Most 16 oz tubs are filled close to the top. If there isn't room, blend the cottage cheese in the tub and finish the dip in a bowl.
- Press plastic wrap onto the surface to slow browning. Best within 2 days.
