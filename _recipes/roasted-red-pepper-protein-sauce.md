## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1/2 cup roasted red peppers (jarred), drained
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Drain the roasted red peppers and pat them dry.
2. Blend the peppers, paprika, garlic powder, olive oil, lemon juice and salt until smooth.
3. Add the yogurt and blend briefly, just until combined.
4. Add the water if needed to loosen it, then taste and adjust the salt.

## Pairs well with

- Grilled chicken and grain bowls
- A spread for wraps, sandwiches and pitas
- Warmed gently and tossed with pasta and chicken (don't boil, or the yogurt will split)
- A dip for raw vegetables or pita chips

## Notes

- The source lists only ingredients; the method is written from them.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
