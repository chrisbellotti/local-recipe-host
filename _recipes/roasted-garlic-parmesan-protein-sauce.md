## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp parmesan cheese, finely grated
- 2 garlic cloves, unpeeled
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Toast the unpeeled garlic cloves in a dry pan over medium-low heat, covered, for 8-10 minutes, turning occasionally, until soft and browned in spots.
2. Let the garlic cool slightly, then peel and mash it into a paste.
3. Whisk the garlic paste into the yogurt with the parmesan, garlic powder, black pepper, lemon juice and salt.
4. Whisk in the water to loosen it, then taste and adjust the salt and pepper.

## Pairs well with

- A dip for chicken tenders or air-fried chicken bites

## Notes

- The source lists only ingredients; the method is written from them.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
