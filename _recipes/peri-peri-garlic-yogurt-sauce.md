## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp peri peri seasoning
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Finely grate the garlic into a small bowl.
2. Add the yogurt, peri peri seasoning, paprika, honey, lemon juice and salt, and whisk until smooth.
3. Whisk in the water to loosen it to a drizzling consistency.
4. Taste and adjust the salt or heat, then chill for 15 minutes so the garlic mellows.

## Pairs well with

- Grilled or air-fried chicken thighs, Nando's style
- Chicken wraps and pitas
- Roasted potatoes or fries, as a dip
- Chicken and rice bowls with charred peppers

## Notes

- The source lists only ingredients; the method is written from them.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
