## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp fresh dill, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Zest of 1 lemon
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Finely chop the dill and zest the lemon.
2. Whisk the yogurt, dill, lemon zest, lemon juice, garlic powder, black pepper and salt until smooth.
3. Whisk in the water to loosen it.
4. Chill for at least 15 minutes so the dill flavors the sauce.

- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
