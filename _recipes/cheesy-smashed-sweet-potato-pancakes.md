## Ingredients

- 4 medium or 6 small sweet potatoes
- 4-6 small handfuls cheddar cheese (or a plant-based cheese)
- Salt and pepper
- 2 eggs
- 1-2 tbsp crispy chilli oil (Lao Gan Ma)

### Garnish

- Creme fraiche or yogurt (or a plant-based alternative)
- Pickled red onions
- More chilli crisp
- Chives or spring onions, sliced

## Instructions

1. Prick the sweet potatoes all over and microwave on high until tender, about 15-20 minutes.
2. Smash each potato flat between two sheets of baking paper with a burger press, spatula or pan. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium-high heat.
4. Add the cheese to the dry pan in small rounds, about the same size as the smashed potatoes.
5. Top each cheese round with a sweet potato and cook until golden and crispy.
6. Flip and cook until golden and crispy on the other side. Work in batches if needed.
7. Wipe the pan clean and heat the chilli oil over medium heat.
8. Crack in the eggs and cook to your liking. Cover with a lid or bowl for the last 15-20 seconds to set the whites on top.
9. Stack the sweet potatoes and top with the egg, creme fraiche, pickled onions, more chilli crisp and chives.

## Notes

- Pesto works in place of the chilli oil; add a little extra oil to fry the egg.
