## Ingredients

- 1/4 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp hot sauce (such as Frank's RedHot)
- 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Add the yogurt, hot sauce, paprika, garlic powder, honey, lemon juice and salt to a small bowl.
2. Whisk until smooth.
3. Whisk in the water to loosen it.
4. Taste and add more hot sauce if you want it spicier.

## Pairs well with

- Buffalo chicken wraps or salads
- Air-fried chicken wings or tenders
- Celery, carrots and cucumber, as a dip
- On the [Crispy Cilantro Lime Honey Chicken Sandwich]({{ '/recipes/cilantro-lime-honey-chicken-sandwich/' | relative_url }}) for a buffalo version

## Notes

- The source lists only ingredients; the method is written from them.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
