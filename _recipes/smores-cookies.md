## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 cup brown sugar
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 1 1/4 cups flour
- 1/2 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 3/4 cup chocolate chips
- Graham cracker sheets, broken into pieces

### Topping

- Mini marshmallows
- Hershey's chocolate bar, broken into pieces

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 350F and line a baking sheet with parchment.
2. Cream the butter, brown sugar and sugar until light and fluffy.
3. Mix in the egg and vanilla.
4. Add the flour, baking soda and salt and mix until just combined.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips and graham cracker pieces.
6. Scoop into large dough balls and space them out on the baking sheet.
7. Press a few mini marshmallows and a piece of Hershey's chocolate into the top of each.
8. Bake for 10-12 minutes, until the edges are golden.

## Notes

- The source doesn't give a yield; 12 large cookies is an estimate.
- Let the cookies cool on the sheet for a few minutes before moving them, since the marshmallows are soft when hot.
