## Ingredients

- 1/2 cup Greek yogurt
- 1 tsp Cajun seasoning
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp water
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions

1. Add the yogurt, Cajun seasoning, garlic powder, onion powder, honey and lemon juice to a small bowl.
2. Whisk until smooth.
3. Whisk in the water to loosen it.
4. Taste before adding salt, since many Cajun blends are already salty.

## Pairs well with

- Blackened shrimp or chicken
- Po' boy-style sandwiches and wraps
- Potato wedges, fries or sweet potato fries
- Rice bowls with sausage, peppers and onions

## Notes

- The source lists only ingredients; the method is written from them.
- Keeps covered in the fridge for up to 5 days. Stir in a splash of water if it thickens.
