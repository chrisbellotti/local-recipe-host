## Ingredients

- 1 tub (16 oz) 2% cottage cheese
- 1/4 cup buffalo sauce (such as Frank's RedHot)
- 1 cup cooked chicken breast, shredded (rotisserie works well)
- 2 green onions, sliced

## Instructions

1. Blend the cottage cheese in its tub with an immersion blender until completely smooth.
2. Stir in the buffalo sauce.
3. Add the shredded chicken and green onions and mix until combined.
4. Taste and add more buffalo sauce if you want it spicier.

## Pairs well with

- Celery, carrots and cucumber
- Tortilla chips or pita chips
- Stuffed into a wrap or lettuce cups for a quick lunch
- Warmed and spooned onto a baked potato

## Notes

- Amounts are estimated; the source only listed the ingredients.
- Serve cold, or warm it in a microwave-safe dish in 30-second bursts, stirring between each.
- Most 16 oz tubs are filled close to the top. If there isn't room, blend in the tub and finish the dip in a bowl.
